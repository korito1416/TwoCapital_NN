### Strong-tier failure cluster — the post-tech regimes' climate/scale directions
- **PT-5 (legs 2–3), CD-2-subA, CD-2-subB, plus the CD-7 marginal and the CD-4 terminal-regime caveat** form one coherent defect: (i) the PreDamagePostTech nets are **Y-degenerate** — the i_d net's Y input is dead (first-layer weight −5.3e-6; slope +2.1e-11) and the value net's raw v_y ≈ −2.8e-6, so the post-tech regime anticipates no warming and no damage-jump hazard at all; (ii) the PostDamagePostTech responses that FD pins are **sign-inverted**: ∂i_d/∂Y positive (FD: −2.9e-2), ∂i_d/∂logK positive (FD: −1.1e-2..−3.4e-2), dq_d/dY emission loading +8.5e-4 vs FD −1.6e-1 (~200x too weak, wrong sign); (iii) the scale-break is mis-oriented in Z (V_logK falls toward Z=1 where the S1 corner anchor requires it to rise to 1).
