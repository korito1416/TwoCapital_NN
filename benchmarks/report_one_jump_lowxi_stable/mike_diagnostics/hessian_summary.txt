==============================================================================
 INTERPRETATION (for the low-xi worst-case density collapse)
==============================================================================
   The constant (level) function is an EXACT eigenvector of the linearized HJB operator
   A = L - delta*I with eigenvalue exactly -delta = -0.01 (verified to 1e-17), and it is
   the SMALLEST-magnitude eigenvalue: the next mode sits at ~1.8*delta and every bulk
   mode carries the O(drift/diffusion) transport scale (|lambda|_max ~ 1.6), hundreds of
   times larger. So A squashes the level/gauge direction to magnitude delta while acting
   O(100x) more stiffly on every mode with spatial structure -- the level is a near-null
   (gauge) direction of the stationary operator. The least-squares HJB loss Hessian ~ A^T A
   inherits this: its smallest singular value is ~delta or, because A is non-normal
   (advection-dominated), even SMALLER (here 6e-4 < delta), so the level is pinned even
   more weakly than -delta alone implies. Concretely the stationary HJB determines the
   value LEVEL only to ~ residual/delta: a residual ~1e-3 leaves the level ambiguous to
   ~1e-1. The damage-jump distortion g = exp(-(1/xi)(V^l - V)) reads this weakly-identified
   ABSOLUTE level, and the prefactor 1/xi amplifies the ~0.1 level ambiguity into an
   O(0.1/xi) error in the exponent -- ~2 at xi=0.05, ~10 at xi=0.01 -- which is exactly why
   the worst-case damage-jump DENSITY (unlike the economics or temperature density, which
   depend on value DIFFERENCES / gradients = the well-identified bulk modes) becomes
   unreliable below xi ~ 0.025. The mechanism is a genuine near-null gauge direction of the
   operator, confirmed to machine precision and shown grid-independent (fine == coarse).
