INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The spectrum CONFIRMS the low-frequency value-LEVEL weak-identification and REFUTES
a second, high-frequency threshold defect. The terminal-regime NN-vs-FD error is
dominated by a near-constant (DC) mode of size ~0.1-0.19 -- exactly the predicted
residual/delta ~ 0.1 level ambiguity that the -delta*V anchor (delta=0.01) leaves
>=91% of the error energy sits in the lowest 2^3 DCT modes; after removing a single
global constant the remaining spread is still ~0.10-0.11 and still low-frequency,
i.e. a slowly-varying gauge drift, not fine structure. This is precisely a level
mode that the cross-regime jump distortion g=exp(-(1/xi)(V^l - V)) amplifies by 1/xi.
Meanwhile the damage threshold at y_lower=1.5 shows NO anomalous high-frequency
power at any xi, and the density collapse is a smooth magnitude decay rather than
NN under-resolution / spectral-bias ringing. Honest caveat: the FD reference is the
SMOKE grid (25x31x25) at lam3=1/6 with econ-box rms residual ~4e-4 (xi=.05) / ~7e-4
(xi=.01); the absolute DC offset carries the FD's own O(1e-3/delta) level uncertainty,
so the ~0.1 magnitude is order-of-magnitude, not 3-digit. The SHAPE of the spectrum
(DC/low-freq dominant, no threshold ringing) is robust to that and is the load-bearing
conclusion.
