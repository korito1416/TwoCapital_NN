The excluded regimes are post-damage/intermediate-tech and pre-damage/intermediate-tech.  The active pre-technology stages use
\[
\pi = 1, \qquad \texttt{tech\_jump\_intensity\_scale}=1.
\]
Thus
\[
J_g^{\prime}(R) = (1-\pi)\frac{R}{\varrho}=0,
\qquad
J_g^{\prime\prime}(R) = \pi\frac{R}{\varrho}=\frac{R}{\varrho}.
\]

The inherited post-technology stage folders may still contain the original two-stage metadata value $\pi=0.04$ in their local \texttt{params.txt}.  This does not affect the post-technology HJBs because those regimes have no remaining technology jump branch.  The active pre-technology stages in this one-jump model use $\pi=1$.

\section{Controls and Resource Constraint}

Let
\[
K_t = K_t^d + K_t^g,
\qquad
Z_t = \frac{K_t^g}{K_t},
\qquad
K_t^d=(1-Z_t)K_t,\quad K_t^g=Z_tK_t.
\]

The control networks report investment rates:
\[
i_t^d = \frac{I_t^d}{K_t^d},
\qquad
i_t^g = \frac{I_t^g}{K_t^g},
\qquad
i_t^r = \frac{I_t^r}{K_t}.
\]
The corresponding investment levels are
\[
I_t^d=i_t^d(1-Z_t)K_t,\qquad
I_t^g=i_t^gZ_tK_t,\qquad
I_t^r=i_t^rK_t.
\]

In pre-technology regimes, R\&D is active and the consumption-output accounting uses
\[
\frac{C_t}{K_t}
=(A_d-i_t^d)(1-Z_t)+(A_g-i_t^g)Z_t-i_t^r.
\]
After the final technology jump, R\&D is inactive and green productivity is $A_g^{\prime\prime}$:
\[
\frac{C_t}{K_t}
=(A_d-i_t^d)(1-Z_t)+(A_g^{\prime\prime}-i_t^g)Z_t.
\]

\section{State Dynamics}

Sectoral capital obeys
\[
dK_t^j
=K_t^j\left[\alpha_j+\Gamma_j\log(1+\theta_j i_t^j)\right]dt
 + \sigma_jK_t^j\,dW_t^j,
\qquad j\in\{d,g\}.
\]
Thus
\[
\phi_j(i)=\alpha_j+\Gamma_j\log(1+\theta_j i),
\qquad
\phi_j'(i)=\frac{\Gamma_j\theta_j}{1+\theta_j i}.
\]

Knowledge capital obeys
\[
d\log R_t
=\left[
-\zeta+\psi_0\exp\{\psi_1(\log i_t^r+\log K_t-\log R_t)\}
-\frac{1}{2}\sigma_\kappa^2
\right]dt+\sigma_\kappa\,dW_t^r
\]
in the deterministic simulation code.  In the HJB residual, the corresponding drift term is implemented with the Ito correction handled in the PDE diffusion block.

Temperature evolves as
\[
dY_t=\bar\theta\,\eta A_d(1-Z_t)K_t\,dt
\]
along deterministic paths, with stochastic volatility loading $\varsigma$ in the HJB.

\section{Specification Checks for the Current Discussion}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The active one-jump technology intensity is $J_g(R)=R/\varrho$, not $R$ directly.  With $\varrho=746.67$, the scaling of $R$ should be checked when interpreting R\&D levels and arrival rates.
\item R\&D investment is reported by the network as a rate $i^r=I^r/K$.  Any reported R\&D level should be checked against $I^r=i^rK$ and against any plotting convention used in the density scripts.
\item Capital volatility is currently $\sigma_d=\sigma_g=0.01$.
\item The adjustment term is implemented as $\Gamma_j\log(1+\theta_j i^j)$.  Therefore $\Gamma_j$ enters the drift and the marginal FOC through $\Gamma_j\theta_j/(1+\theta_j i^j)$.  Before interpreting a lower $\Gamma_j$ as a lower adjustment cost, we should verify the intended normalization because lowering $\Gamma_j$ also lowers the marginal contribution of investment to capital growth in this implementation.
\item The R\&D productivity parameter is currently $\psi_0=0.10583$.  Increasing $\psi_0$ raises the effectiveness of R\&D in the $\log R$ drift and in the R\&D FOC.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Proposed Sensitivity Runs}

For the next check, the natural sensitivity cases are:
\[
(\sigma_d,\sigma_g)\downarrow,\qquad
(\Gamma_d,\Gamma_g)\downarrow \text{ after confirming interpretation},\qquad
\psi_0\uparrow.
\]
Each sensitivity should be run holding the one-jump structure fixed:
\[
\pi=1,\qquad J_g(R)=R/\varrho,\qquad
A_g\rightarrow A_g^{\prime\prime}.
\]
